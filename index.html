      Straight answers.

      Where does it live?
      Anywhere indoors and dry. A basement shelf, the hall closet, under the stairs. The cases stack on a footprint the size of a storage tote.
      Is two weeks of water really in there?
      No, and no pack honestly can be. Two weeks of water for four people weighs over 450 pounds. Each Ration case holds sealed water for the first three days. The Core holds a filter and carriers for the rest, and the Water store module adds four tanks.
      What if I never use it?
      Then it did its job. So did your smoke alarm.
      What if my household changes?
      Add a Ration case. One more person is one more case, and it stacks with the rest.
